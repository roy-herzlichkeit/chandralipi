## 3. What pushed it over (in order of size)

1. **The human widened the TMC-2 and IIRS picks beyond the LLD's limits.**
   - LLD §2.1 rows 5–6 allow at most 2 TMC-2 and 1 IIRS product.
   - The human chose 4 TMC-2 files (2 calibrated plus the derived ortho and DTM) and 2 IIRS products.
   - The extra files (ortho + DTM + second IIRS product, unpacked) come to **22.6 GB**. That alone exceeds the 20 GB budget. **M** (row sums) · **R** (transcript)
2. **The zips are kept alongside the unpacked copies** (LLD §1). That is 10.9 GB of duplication. **M** · **R**
3. **The TMC-2 derived ortho is stored uncompressed.** Its zip is 432 890 957 B, but the unpacked file is 14.7 GB, a 34:1 ratio. **M**
